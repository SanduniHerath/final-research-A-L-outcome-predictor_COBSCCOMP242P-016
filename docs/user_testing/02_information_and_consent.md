## Information sheet (for parents and students)

**Project:** Alora – an app that helps students who have just finished O/L to plan for A/L (Science stream).
**Researcher:** [name], [degree programme], [university]. **Supervisor:** [name, email].
**Ethics approval:** [reference number].

**What is this about?** I am a university student. For my final-year project I built an app called Alora. It estimates likely A/L results from answers about the O/L years, compares them with university cut-offs, and suggests a study plan. I want to find out if students like you find it easy to use and helpful.

**What will happen?**
- **One session of about 45 minutes** at [place], with me present.
- **You will try the app on a laptop** and do some simple tasks, like "find your likely Z-score". You will say out loud what you think as you use it.
- **Then you will fill in a short questionnaire** about the app.
- **You can use your own O/L answers or a made-up example student.** It is your choice.

**Is it private?**
- **Your answers stay on the laptop** and are deleted at the end of the session. They are not sent anywhere.
- **The questionnaire has a code (like "T3"), not your name.**
- **Only I and my supervisor see the questionnaires.** They are stored safely and destroyed after [date].
- **Your name will not appear in my thesis.**

**Is it a test of me?** No. We are testing the app, not you. There are no right or wrong answers.

**What are the risks?** Very small. The app might show a lower result than you hoped. It is only an estimate from other students' answers, and it does not decide your future. You can switch to an example student or stop at any time.

**Do I have to take part?** No. It is completely voluntary. You can stop at any time, without giving a reason, and nothing bad will happen.

**Questions?** Contact [researcher email / phone] or the supervisor [email].
