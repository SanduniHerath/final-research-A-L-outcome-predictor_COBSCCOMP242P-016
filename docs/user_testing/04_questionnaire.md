## Part B – Understanding "Why This Result"

Circle **one** answer for each question.

**B1.** On the "Why This Result" page, a **blue bar with ▲** next to a group of answers means:
- a) you answered those questions correctly
- b) that group of answers **raised** your predicted Z-score compared with a typical student
- c) those subjects are your best subjects in A/L

**B2.** The app says: *"Your O/L results lowered your predicted Z-score by about 0.15."* This means:
- a) your A/L result will be exactly 0.15 lower
- b) your O/L grades were marked wrongly
- c) compared with a typical student in the survey, your O/L results are linked to a prediction about 0.15 lower

**B3.** Look at your own "Why This Result" page. Which group of answers moved your predicted Z-score **the most**?
Write it here: ________________________________ *(the researcher checks it against your screen)*

**B4.** Does the "Why This Result" page **prove** that your study habits **cause** your Z-score?
- a) Yes, it proves it
- b) No. It shows patterns in other students' answers, not proof of cause
- c) Only for students with good results

**B5.** Which of these can you still **work on** to improve, according to the app?
- a) your O/L grades
- b) your study habits
- c) the type of school you went to

**B6.** The app compares you with a **"typical student"**. This is:
- a) an average student from the survey the app learned from
- b) the best student in your class
- c) a student from your own school

**B7.** Under "Why [degree area]?", a bar says **▲ +40** for "Your A/L subject choice". This means:
- a) you need 40 more marks in A/L
- b) your A/L subject choice raised the chance of that degree area by about 40 percentage points
- c) 40 students chose the same subjects

**B8.** If some single answers seem to point the "wrong" way, what does the app tell you to do?
- a) Look at the groups of answers first
- b) Answer the questions again
- c) Ignore the whole page
