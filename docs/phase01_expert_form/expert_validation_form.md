# Expert content-validity form

Each question is rated from 1 to 4 for relevance, clarity and simplicity.

**Relevance:** 1 = Not relevant; 2 = Somewhat relevant (needs major change); 3 = Quite relevant (needs minor change); 4 = Highly relevant

**Clarity:** 1 = Not clear; 2 = Needs major revision to be clear; 3 = Clear but needs minor revision; 4 = Very clear

**Simplicity:** 1 = Not simple; 2 = Needs major revision to be simple; 3 = Simple but needs minor revision; 4 = Very simple

## Background for the expert

- Questions 14-17 measure academic self-efficacy during O/L (adapted from the MSLQ, Pintrich et al., 1991).
- Questions 18-21 measure effort regulation and time management during O/L (adapted from the MSLQ). Question 21 is negatively worded on purpose.
- Questions 22-25 measure interest in and value of Science and Mathematics (developed for this study).
- Other questions are factual (background, grades, activities, outcomes) and are used as single items, not as scales.
- Respondents answer about their own past (O/L and A/L years); they are now undergraduates.
